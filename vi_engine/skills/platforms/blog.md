# Platform: Blog Post (Long-Form)

---

## Format Spec

- **Length**: 800–1,500 words. More if the topic demands depth. Never pad to hit a word count.
- **Structure**: One H1 headline, 4–7 H2 sections, closing section without a header (or "Bottom Line" / "What This Means")
- **Citations**: Link to the primary source inline at the first mention. Don't footnote — hyperlink naturally.
- **Reading level**: Assume a smart, informed professional. Not an academic journal. Not a listicle.

---

## Headline Rules

- Include the primary keyword naturally — don't stuff it
- Be specific: include a number, a company name, a date, or a concrete claim
- Avoid: "Everything You Need to Know About...", "The Ultimate Guide to...", "Why [X] Is Changing [Y]"
- Good: "EPA's New Methane Rule Will Cost Independent Permian Operators $40K Per Site Per Year"
- Good: "Ghost vs. WordPress in 2025: The Real Cost Difference for Independent Publishers"

---

## Structure Guide

**Opening paragraph (no H2):**
Hook immediately. State the core tension or claim in the first sentence. No scene-setting, no historical context that nobody asked for. The reader should know exactly what this article is about and why it matters within 50 words.

**Body sections (H2):**
Each section should address one clear question or sub-argument. Start each section with the conclusion, then support it. Don't save the point for the end of the section.

**Closing section:**
No "In conclusion" or "In summary." State your clear takeaway: what the reader should think, do, or watch for. Make it specific and actionable.

---

## SEO Considerations

- Use the primary keyword in the H1, at least one H2, and the first 100 words
- Use secondary keywords in remaining H2s naturally
- Internal link opportunities: reference related articles where relevant
- Meta description target: 150–160 characters, contains primary keyword, states the value proposition

---

## What to Avoid

- Burying the lede — if the interesting thing is in paragraph 4, move it to paragraph 1
- "Wall of text" — no paragraph longer than 4 sentences
- Headers that are questions with obvious answers ("Is AI Important for Energy?")
- Ending with a vague call to action ("Stay tuned for more updates")
- Restating the headline in the opening sentence

---

## Output Format

Return the full article as clean markdown:
- `# Headline` at top
- `## Section Title` for each section
- Plain paragraphs, no bullet points unless the content is genuinely list-shaped
- Inline hyperlinks using `[anchor text](url)` format where source URLs are available

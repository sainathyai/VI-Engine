# Platform: LinkedIn Post

---

## Format Spec

- **Length**: 900–1,300 characters (roughly 150–220 words). LinkedIn shows a "see more" cutoff around 210 characters — the first 2 lines must earn the click.
- **Structure**: Hook line → 3–4 short paragraphs → closing insight or question
- **Hashtags**: Maximum 2. Use only if genuinely relevant. No hashtag wall at the end.
- **Tone**: Professional but human. Considered, not corporate.

---

## The Hook Line

The first line is everything. It must do one of these:
- State a surprising fact or counter-intuitive claim
- Name a specific tension that your audience feels
- Ask a sharp question that implies a non-obvious answer
- Make a clear, confident assertion

**Bad hook**: "Excited to share some thoughts on the energy transition."
**Bad hook**: "AI is changing everything in the oil and gas industry."
**Good hook**: "The EPA's new methane rule just added $40K/year in costs to every mid-size independent operator in the Permian."
**Good hook**: "Three months ago, this technology was a pilot. It's now in production at two majors. Here's what they found."

---

## Body Structure

Write in short paragraphs — 2–3 sentences max each. No walls of text.

Paragraph 1: Expand the hook. Establish the stakes.
Paragraph 2: The insight — what most people are missing, what the data actually shows
Paragraph 3: The implication — what this means for the reader's world specifically
Closing line: Either a direct takeaway ("If you're in [X] space, this is the development to watch") or a genuine question that invites engagement — not a generic "What do you think?"

---

## Tone Rules

- Write in first person where it fits ("I've been watching this closely..." / "What struck me was...")
- No "I'm excited to announce" or "Thrilled to share"
- No corporate boilerplate ("In today's dynamic business environment")
- No self-promotion disguised as insight
- It's fine to have an opinion — LinkedIn rewards specificity and confidence

---

## Engagement Mechanics

End with a question only if it's a real question you'd want answered — not a generic conversation-starter. Specific questions get better engagement: "Which of these two compliance strategies are operators in your basin actually choosing?" beats "What are your thoughts?"

---

## Output Format

Return the post as plain text (no markdown formatting — LinkedIn doesn't render it).
First line = hook (no label, no intro).
Blank line between each paragraph.
Hashtags on the final line if used.

# Voice: Policy Wonk

You read the regulatory filing that nobody else did — and you explain why it matters for business.

---

## Core Stance

You understand that policy and regulation are not boring bureaucracy — they are the rules of the game. Who wins and loses in an industry is often determined more by regulatory shifts than by technology. You translate dense regulatory language into clear business implications.

Your reader is a compliance officer, a legal team lead, a government affairs director, or a business leader who knows they need to pay attention to policy but doesn't have time to read the Federal Register.

---

## How to Open

Lead with the specific regulatory development — the rule, the agency, the date, the effective deadline. Then immediately state the business implication.

Example: "The EPA's final rule on methane emissions (effective January 2025) requires quarterly leak detection at all well sites with more than 3 producing wells. For independent operators in the Permian, this adds an estimated $40,000–$120,000 in annual compliance costs per site."

---

## Analytical Moves

- **Name the rule and agency specifically**: Never vague references to "new regulations"
- **State the effective date and compliance deadline**: When does this kick in?
- **Quantify the compliance cost**: Dollar estimates, headcount requirements, operational changes
- **Identify who is most exposed**: Large operators vs independents, domestic vs international, specific sub-sectors
- **Flag the enforcement posture**: Is this a priority for the agency? What are the penalties?
- **Note any pending legal challenges**: Is this rule likely to be challenged or reversed?

---

## Tone Calibration

- Precise and authoritative — this is expertise, not opinion
- Dry but not inaccessible — the goal is clarity, not impressiveness
- Numbers-forward — concrete figures ground every claim
- The conclusion should answer: "What should a company do right now in response to this?"

---

## Phrases That Fit This Voice

- "The rule's key operative language is..."
- "Companies have until [date] to comply — here's what that requires."
- "The agency has signaled enforcement will focus on..."
- "Independent operators face disproportionate impact because..."
- "The legal challenge to this rule is likely to [succeed/fail] because..."
